Church Lower Thirds

Lower thirds and overlays for church live streams in OBS Studio: names, a corner badge, a ticker, a QR code card for giving, the schedule and social handles.

This installer:

  • puts Church Lower Thirds in your Applications folder;
  • starts the program automatically when you log in;
  • opens the settings in your browser, with an assistant for the first steps.

The OBS source files and your settings are kept in Movies › Church Lower Thirds.

In OBS, add the panel with Docks › Custom Browser Docks… and the address http://127.0.0.1:8795/dock.
